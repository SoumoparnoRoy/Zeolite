Zeolite

Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

Zeolite is an attendance tracker for students, published by Zeolite. This policy describes what the app does with your information, who else can see it, how long it is kept and how to have it deleted.

Zeolite works without an account, and in that state nothing you enter leaves your device. Signing in is optional, and it is what changes the answer — so this policy is written in two halves, with the measurement that applies to both set out between them.

If you do not sign in

Everything you create — subjects, teachers, rooms, your timetable, attendance marks, tags, notes and holidays — is written to a database inside the app's own private storage on your device. It is not uploaded, and the developer cannot see it.

Two things still leave the device even without an account, and neither carries anything you entered: the usage measurement described below, and the ML Kit diagnostics described further down.

One more thing can leave, but only if you ask for it. Connecting Notion does not require an account either, and once you connect it your attendance is written to your own Notion workspace. Nothing is sent there unless you connect it, and disconnecting stops it. That is described under Connecting Notion below.

If you sign in

Signing in creates a Zeolite account so your attendance survives a lost phone and stays the same on a second device. It is optional, off until you choose it, and it never gates recording attendance.

What is collected

Your email address
To identify your account and let you sign back in. Held in Firebase Authentication, operated by Google.
Your subjects, timetable, attendance marks, categories, rooms, tags, holidays, and the app settings that shape your term
To back them up and keep two devices in step. Held in Cloud Firestore, operated by Google, under a record keyed to your account.

If you sign in with Google, Google tells Zeolite your email address and basic profile information so the account can be created. Zeolite never sees your Google password.

Usage measurement, whether or not you sign in

Zeolite uses Firebase Analytics, from Google, to count how the app is used so its developer can tell whether it works for people. This is collected on every install, account or not, and it is the one thing the app reports without being asked.

What it records is how the app is used, not what you put into it: when the app is opened, how long sessions last, the app version, and coarse device and country information derived from your connection. It never includes your timetable, your attendance, your subjects, your notes, or the contents of any image you read.

The advertising identifier is switched off. Zeolite sets google_analytics_adid_collection_enabled to false and strips the advertising permissions from the app, so this measurement is not tied to an advertising ID and cannot be used to target you.

What is not collected

There is no advertising, no crash reporting, and no tracking of you across other apps or websites. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone for marketing.

Who can access it

Your records are stored under your own account and the security rules permit only that account to read or write them. Google processes and stores the data as the infrastructure provider under its own terms. The developer can technically reach the stored records through the project's administrative console, and does so only where necessary to operate or repair the service.

Reading a timetable from an image

If you use “Read from an image”, the picture is processed on your device by Google's ML Kit text recognition, using a model bundled inside the app. The image is not uploaded, not kept after the read, and not shared.

One side effect is worth stating plainly. ML Kit ships a component that reports usage and performance metrics about the ML Kit APIs to Google, and it provides no setting to switch this off. It carries diagnostic information about the app and the device — not your timetable, your attendance, or the images you read.

Connecting Notion

Notion sync is optional and off by default, and it does not require a Zeolite account. If you turn it on:

  • You authorise Zeolite from Notion's own sign-in page and choose which pages or databases it may reach. Zeolite never sees your Notion password.
  • Your attendance is written to the Notion database you picked, inside your own workspace. What happens to it there is governed by Notion's privacy policy, not this one.
  • The sign-in exchange passes through a small service the developer runs, which exists only because Notion requires a secret that cannot safely be shipped inside an app. It completes the token exchange, holds the exchange in memory for at most five minutes, and keeps no copy of your attendance.
  • The connection is stored encrypted on your device and is never uploaded. Signing out of a Zeolite account does not disconnect Notion, because the two are unrelated.
  • Disconnecting stops any further sync and leaves everything in the app intact.

Backups on your device

  • Manual export writes a backup file where you choose. What you then do with that file is your own action.
  • Automatic backup writes to a folder you pick, or to the app's own folder, on the same device.
  • Android cloud backup is switched off. Zeolite sets allowBackup="false" and excludes itself from device-to-device transfer, so the operating system does not copy your data to Google Drive or to a new phone.

Who your data reaches

Google — Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore
Only if you sign in. Stores your email address and the records you chose to back up, as the infrastructure provider.
Google — Firebase Analytics
Always. Receives the usage measurement described above, and nothing you entered.
Google — ML Kit
Only if you read from an image. The recognition happens on your device; only diagnostics about the API reach Google.
Notion
Only if you connect it. Receives the attendance you chose to sync, into your own workspace.

No one else. Your data is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising.

How long it is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Data in your account stays until you delete it. There is no separate archive: deleting removes the records rather than hiding them.

How to delete it

  • On the device — Settings → Reset everything clears all subjects and history. Uninstalling removes the database with it.
  • Your account — Settings → Backup and sync → Delete my account removes the account and everything stored under it.
  • Without the app installed — see deleting your account.
  • Notion — disconnecting stops further sync. Records already written to your workspace are yours to delete in Notion.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of the personal data held about you, ask for it to be corrected, ask for it to be deleted, or withdraw your consent to it being stored by deleting your account. Most of this you can do yourself in the app; for anything you cannot, write to the address below and it will be actioned.

If you are in India and believe your data has been mishandled, you may raise a grievance at the address below and, if unsatisfied, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

Zeolite is a general-purpose study tool. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Traffic between the app and your account is encrypted in transit. Stored records are held in Google's infrastructure and are restricted by rules that permit only your own account to read or write them. No system is perfect, and this policy does not promise one.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the updated version is published at this address.
