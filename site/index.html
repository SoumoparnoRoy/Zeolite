Zeolite

Attendance, tracked properly.

Zeolite records the classes on your timetable and the ones you actually attended, then tells you where you stand against the attendance you need — how many you can still afford to miss, and how many in a row it takes to climb back.

How it works

  • Offline by default. Everything lives on your device. There is no account until you want one, and marking attendance never needs a connection.
  • Your timetable, once. Enter it, paste it, or read it from a photo — the text recognition runs on the device.
  • Backup and a second device. Sign in and your records are kept safe and stay the same everywhere you use them.
  • Notion, if you use it. Sync your attendance into a database in your own Notion workspace.
