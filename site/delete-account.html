Deleting your Zeolite account

Last updated 5 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your Zeolite account and the data stored with it. You do not need the app installed to request it.

In the app, if you still have it

Open Settings → Backup and sync → Account → Delete my account and confirm. This is immediate.

By email, if you do not

Write to zeolite.help@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, asking for your Zeolite account to be deleted. Sending it from that address is what lets us confirm the account is yours; if you cannot, say so and we will find another way to verify it.

Requests are actioned within 30 days, and usually much sooner. You will get a reply confirming when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your Zeolite account and the email address held with it.
  • Everything stored under it: your subjects, timetable, attendance marks, categories, rooms, tags, holidays and the synced app settings.

Nothing is kept in an archive, and nothing is retained after deletion.

What is not deleted, and why

  • The copy on your own device. Deleting the account does not wipe your phone. Use Settings → Reset everything, or uninstall the app.
  • Backup files you exported. Those are yours, wherever you put them.
  • Anything already written into your own Notion workspace. Those pages belong to your Notion account and are yours to delete there.

Usage statistics and crash reports

If you agreed to Share usage statistics or Send crash reports, what was sent is not linked to your account, so deleting the account does not remove it. Google deletes it on its own schedule:

  • Usage statistics are kept for up to two months after you last use the app, then deleted by Google.
  • Crash reports are kept by Google for 90 days.

To stop either, turn it off in Settings → Privacy choices; nothing more is sent from then on.

Using Zeolite without an account

Deleting your account does not stop you using the app. Zeolite records attendance with no account at all — you simply lose the backup and the ability to keep two devices in step.
